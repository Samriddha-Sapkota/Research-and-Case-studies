# Research & Case Studies

A curated collection of cybersecurity research, technical case studies, and academic investigations conducted throughout my undergraduate studies.

The repository documents my exploration of real-world cybersecurity incidents, vulnerabilities, security controls, professional ethics, and emerging areas of information security.

## Cybersecurity

### APT1 & Lockheed Martin

**Focus:** Advanced Persistent Threats, cyber espionage, supply-chain exposure, and defensive security.

The study examines the APT1 threat, its reported activity against defence-related organisations, and the lessons that can be drawn around detection, network segmentation, incident response, and supplier security.

[View Case Study](./Cybersecurity/Apt1-Lockheed-Martin/)

---

### Jeep Cherokee Uconnect

**Focus:** Automotive cybersecurity, connected vehicles, CAN bus security, ethics, and regulation.

The study examines the 2015 Jeep Cherokee/Uconnect security research and explores the technical, ethical, legal, and social implications of vulnerabilities in connected and safety-critical systems.

[View Case Study](./Cybersecurity/Jeep-Cherokee-Uconnect/)

---

### Software Supply Chain Security

**Focus:** SBOMs, software provenance, SLSA-style controls, SSDF, and software supply-chain risk.

The research examines how vulnerabilities or malicious changes introduced upstream can affect downstream software consumers, and evaluates layered approaches for improving visibility, provenance, and secure software development.

[View Case Study](./Cybersecurity/Software-Supply-Chain-Security/)

## Purpose

This repository serves as a research portfolio rather than a collection of generic coursework files.

Each case study is intended to demonstrate my ability to:

- Research cybersecurity incidents
- Analyse technical security issues
- Identify vulnerabilities and attack surfaces
- Evaluate defensive controls
- Connect technical issues with organisational risk
- Consider ethical and professional responsibilities
- Examine legal and regulatory implications
- Communicate technical findings through structured reports

## Academic Work

The material in this repository was produced primarily as part of undergraduate academic work.

The original reports are retained alongside concise portfolio-oriented README files to preserve the underlying research and analysis.

## Disclaimer

The material in this repository represents academic research and analysis conducted during my undergraduate studies.

It should not be interpreted as professional security assessments, penetration-testing reports, incident-response reports, or claims of professional experience.

Technical, legal, regulatory, and threat information may change over time. Readers should consult current authoritative sources when using the material for operational or professional purposes.
